## Limits and rollout

This is clean reconstruction with complete current instructions, not a claim of
exact replay of every historical context channel. Digest-only or expired input
cannot reconstruct a baseline. Unknown dispatched attempts and mutations remain
explicit reconciliation gaps. The 8 KiB summary limit refuses overflow.

Costs are operator-priced reservations over serialized provider-request bytes,
not actual billing or complete input/output token accounting. An exact token cap
is unavailable at this seam and explicitly refuses execution when requested.
The wall deadline blocks new admissions; already-dispatched transport retains its
own timeout. Retained input has logical expiry and is purged on the root's next
operation, not a physical-erasure guarantee. These limits are not promoted into
claims of measured task-quality or efficiency improvements.
