## Pending deliverables and gates

1. MR-07 enforcement: independent frozen code-navigation tasks, supported model/token accounting, explicit estimated-cost basis and powered paired quality/latency/cost noninferiority. Observe acceptance does not satisfy this gate; operator promotion was deferred.
2. MR-09 adaptive ranking: representative traffic and adequately powered held-out pairs. The six-training/six-held-out pilot selected all arms with zero exposure and established no lift. Decision-time propensity, grouped feedback and promotion are owned by [the consolidated ranking proposal](retrieval-ranking-feedback-and-promotion.md), not duplicated here.
3. MR-08/10 optional collection and utility policies: establish domain-specific baselines, tune only on training splits, retain clock/correction invalidation, document rollback and qualify before activation.
4. MR-15 automatic host-execution procedure attribution and fitted cost policy: supply a real adapter and provenance-bound outcomes; current reporting is observe-only.
5. MR-16 external action adapters beyond the documented local-file adapters: scoped identity, exact destination/effect binding, idempotency, ambiguous-outcome handling and adversarial integration acceptance for each adapter.
6. MR-17 clean retry: live-model quality and cost qualification before promotion. Exact token-cap enforcement is unsupported; do not present estimated costs as billed cost or the controlled provider fixture as live quality.
7. MR-18 ongoing paired live quality/latency/cost and semantic trajectory measurement: invariant gates and CT109 upgrade acceptance are already implemented; they do not establish production benefit.

Acceptance retains the frozen MR-18 policy: complete manifests, at least 30 independent pairs, 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples, explicit seeds/models/budgets/flags/cost basis, observed risk/coverage and refusal of incomplete/underpowered promotion. Preserve optional defaults until the relevant gate passes. Conversational production qualification is also covered by [conversational memory](conversational-memory-and-human-interaction.md).
