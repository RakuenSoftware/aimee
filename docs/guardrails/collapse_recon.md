## Verified typed delta envelope and block lifecycle

The source of truth is `src/headers/aimee_ir.h:186-210`. `aimee_delta_type_t` contains `AIMEE_DELTA_TURN_START`, `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_START`, `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA`, `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_STOP`, `AIMEE_DELTA_TURN_STOP`, and `AIMEE_DELTA_ERROR` (`:188-193`). `aimee_delta_t` is defined at `:196-210`, with `type`, `block_id`, `kind`, `tool_id`, `tool_name`, `text_delta`, `tool_args_delta`, `stop_reason`, `usage_in`, `usage_out`, and `error_message` members.

Text is not a standalone delta type: it is carried in `text_delta` on `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA`, between `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_START` and `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_STOP`. The producer assignments are at `src/server/aimee_ir_stream.c:75-155`, and rendering handles block start/delta/stop at `:428`, `:450`, and `:470`. Phase 2's `/v1/messages` tap must inspect `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA` and preserve lifecycle events; the divergent surfaces require handler-specific adapters that yield equivalent text segments. Invented constants such as `AIMEE_DELTA_TEXT`, `AIMEE_DELTA_TOOL_CALL`, and `AIMEE_DELTA_TURN_END` are rejected.

## Relay choke points

There is no single relay choke point. Phase 2 splits at the emitter/consumer sites in the table. For `/v1/messages`, the exact typed producer is `openai_chunk_to_deltas` (`src/server/aimee_ir_stream.c:42`) and consumer is `anthropic_delta_emit` (`:539`); wire `text_delta` rendering is in the `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA` case at `:450-464`. All other surfaces are divergent and require per-handler adapters with their own tap points cited above.
