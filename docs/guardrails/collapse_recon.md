OpenAI Chat, Responses, webchat, delegate, and roundtable do not all traverse that seam, so it cannot be represented as a repository-wide choke point.

## Verified typed delta envelope and block lifecycle

The source of truth is `src/headers/aimee_ir.h:186-209`. `aimee_delta_type_t` contains `AIMEE_DELTA_TURN_START`, `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_START`, `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA`, `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_STOP`, `AIMEE_DELTA_TURN_STOP`, and `AIMEE_DELTA_ERROR` (`:188-193`). `aimee_delta_t` is defined at `:196-209`, with `type`, `block_id`, `kind`, `tool_id`, `tool_name`, `text_delta`, `tool_args_delta`, `stop_reason`, `usage_in`, `usage_out`, and `error_message` members.

Text is not a standalone delta type: it is carried in `text_delta` on `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA`, between `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_START` and `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_STOP`. The producer assignments are at `src/server/aimee_ir_stream.c:75-155`, and rendering handles block start/delta/stop at `:428`, `:450`, and `:470`. Phase 2's `/v1/messages` tap must inspect `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA` and preserve lifecycle events; the divergent surfaces require handler-specific adapters that yield equivalent text segments. Invented constants such as `AIMEE_DELTA_TEXT`, `AIMEE_DELTA_TOOL_CALL`, and `AIMEE_DELTA_TURN_END` are rejected.

The five divergent surfaces above do not enumerate delta/block values because **no `aimee_delta_type_t` constants are emitted or consumed on those paths** (`grep -n "AIMEE_DELTA_" src/server/openai_chat.c src/server/server_http.c src/db1/webchat_live.c src/server/agent_runtime.c src/server/agent_request_build.c src/modules/workflows/wfe_live_panel.c src/modules/roundtable/delegate_ensemble.c` returns no hits for delta producers; the only producer/consumer site for `aimee_delta_t` is `src/server/aimee_ir_stream.c` and `src/server/anthropic_http.c:697-703`). Phase 2.1 and Phase 2.2 must therefore extract a per-surface replacement representation, each cited in the table above, instead of assuming a unified enum.

- Defaults: `src/modules/config/config.c:601-826`; flags use flat `*_enabled` members and explicit defaults.
- Loader: `config_load`, `src/modules/config/config.c:1109`.

- Template: there is no external template; section descriptions and Markdown assembly are in `scripts/gen-reference-docs.py:340-453`.

**Decision:** Phase 1 adds fields to `config_t` and the existing config section machinery under `guardrails.collapse.*`. The current generator covers those sources, so no Phase 1.0 generator prerequisite is needed.

## Relay choke points

There is no single relay choke point. Phase 2 splits at the emitter/consumer sites in the table. For `/v1/messages`, the exact typed producer is `openai_chunk_to_deltas` (`src/server/aimee_ir_stream.c:42`) and consumer is `anthropic_delta_emit` (`:539`); wire `text_delta` rendering is in the `AIMEE_DELTA_BLOCK_DELTA` case at `:450-464`. All other surfaces are divergent and require per-handler adapters with their own tap points cited above.
